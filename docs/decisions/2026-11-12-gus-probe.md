# GUS probe (read-only)

**Date:** 2026-11-12. A one-time look at what GUS could give the app. Nothing was added to the app.

## What ran

- Skill: AI Expert Suite `sfcli` plugin, `skills/gus/SKILL.md` and `query.md` (version `aisuite.dcd62aff0c1741ed`).
- `sf org display --target-org gus`: connected to `https://gus.my.salesforce.com`, API 68.0. Only those fields were printed.
- One SOQL query: the signed-in user's three most recently changed `ADM_Work__c` items (`Id, Name, Status__c, Type__c, Story_Points__c, Sprint__r.Name, Sprint__r.Start_Date__c, Sprint__r.End_Date__c, Sprint__r.Scrum_Team__r.Name`), matched by `Assignee__r.Email`. It succeeded and returned 0 records. The email was derived from the username as the skill does, which may not match how this person's work is assigned.
- A schema describe of `ADM_Work__c` (metadata only, no records) to look for sprint, utilization, and KSO fields.

## What came back

- **Sprint:** yes. `Sprint__c` (reference to `ADM_Sprint__c`), `Sprint_Name__c`, `Sprint_Rank__c`, and `Sprint_Timeframe__c`. The query above also accepted the `Sprint__r` start, end, and team fields.
- **Utilization:** no utilization field on `ADM_Work__c`. The closest are `Capacity_Impact__c` and `Capacity_Cost_Impact__c`. Capacity-planning objects exist elsewhere (`CaPAD_*`, `CaPlan_*`, `FOX_Capacity_*`), none read.
- **KSO:** no KSO field on `ADM_Work__c`, and no custom object with KSO in its name.

No tokens, record ids, or work-item text are in this doc.
