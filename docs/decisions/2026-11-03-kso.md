# KSOs

**Date:** 2026-11-03. **PRs (on the earlier repo):** #41 (framework and matching), #42 (page and pay), #43 (monthly estimate).

## Decided

- **The framework is read, not written into the code.** KSO material is internal and changes every year. `kso connect` reads the deck and canvases, and one Claude answer turns them into a fixed shape that the code checks. One row per fiscal year. A new year adds a row. The repo only has a made-up framework, for tests and the practice notebook.
- **V2MOM methods are matched to KSO parts.** Managers rate KSOs against the V2MOM, so the V2MOM's own progress is the measure. Claude's matches are marked AI. Your picks stay. If a part's number is gone after a re-read, its methods go back to be matched, including ones you picked.
- **The bars are plain math. The rating is a labeled guess.** A part's bar is the average progress of its methods. A part with no V2MOM goal counts as 0, because a missing one counts against you. The 1 to 5 estimate needs judgment against the rubric, so it is Claude's, marked AI, once a month. Your own rating always wins.
- **Only the KSO share is projected.** Utilization and the target pay are not available here, so you type the target once a year. It stays on this computer and is never sent to Claude.
- **Each year stands alone.** Everything is keyed by the fiscal year from the V2MOM. Methods wait for that year's KSOs. The page shows the newest KSOs while the new year's deck is still pending.

## Rollback

Revert the three PRs. The `kso_*` tables are harmless if unused, the sync skips the estimate without KSOs, and nothing else depends on them.
