# KSOs

**Date:** 2026-11-03. **PRs (on the earlier repo):** #41 (framework and matching), #42 (page and pay), #43 (monthly estimate).

## Decided

- **The framework is read, not written into the code, and stays in the notebook.** The KSO material is internal and changes every year, so `kso connect` reads it (deck and canvases through the connectors) and one Claude answer turns it into a fixed, strictly checked shape. One row per fiscal year; a new year adds a row. The repo only has a made-up framework for tests and the practice notebook.
- **V2MOM methods are matched to KSO parts.** Managers rate KSOs against the V2MOM, so the V2MOM's own progress is the honest measure. Claude matches (marked AI); your picks stay, except that a part whose number is gone after a re-read sends its methods back to be matched, even ones you picked.
- **The bars are plain math; the rating is a labeled guess.** A part's bar is the average progress of its methods, and a part with no V2MOM goal counts as 0, because the rating counts a missing one against you. The 1-5 estimate needs judgment against the rubric, so it's Claude's, marked AI, monthly, and your own rating always wins.
- **Only the KSO share is projected.** Utilization (the rest of the plan) and the target incentive aren't reachable from here, so you type the target once a year. It stays local and is never sent to Claude.
- **Yearly by fiscal year.** Everything is keyed by fiscal year from the V2MOM. Methods wait for their own year's KSOs, and the page shows the newest KSOs while the new year's deck is pending.

## Rollback

Revert the three PRs. The `kso_*` tables are harmless if unused, the sync skips the estimate without KSOs, and nothing else depends on them.
