How this works

Everything coming in from Slack, Google, and GitHub lands here to be sorted. Scan each row and fix what's off with one click: its type, what it counts as, the piece of work it's part of, the leveling guide items it shows, the V2MOM methods it moves forward, the Values & Behaviors it shows, and whether it's a win. On a grouped row, Count this work as one win makes one win for that whole piece of work. Purple AI marks are Claude's picks. They already count on the Performance map. Looks right makes a row's picks yours and moves it out of the "Not checked yet" list. Tick several rows and press Looks right for selected to check them together; a row Claude has not read yet is skipped. No V2MOM method lists records Claude has read that aren't linked to a method. Remove takes a record out for good, for example a meeting you didn't attend; syncs won't bring it back, and you can restore it from "Removed" in the first menu.
